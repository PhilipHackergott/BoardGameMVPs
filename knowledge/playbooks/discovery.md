# Discovery Playbook

What kinds of board games adapt well to a digital MVP — accumulated so the scout's rubric gets
sharper over time.

> Seeded with priors; confirm against real outcomes via `/learn`.

## Working priors (unconfirmed)
- Abstracts and low-component games adapt cleanly (small state space, little art needed).
- Games with heavy bookkeeping/upkeep benefit most from digitization (the computer removes the chore).
- Strong solo/vs-AI potential matters more than peak player count for an MVP.
- "Popular but no good app exists" is the sweet spot for demand vs. competition.
- Dexterity, social-deduction, and negotiation games usually lose their essence on a single screen.
