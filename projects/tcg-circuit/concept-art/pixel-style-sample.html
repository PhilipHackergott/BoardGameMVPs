Pixel-Art Style Target — high-res, GBA era

The finish/texture target for TCG Circuit. References: Golden Sun · Pokémon FRLG/Emerald · Fire Emblem GBA · Advance Wars.
Hand-placed pixels · limited palettes · nearest-neighbour upscale (no blur). These are direction samples, not final assets.

Overworld scene — the look you walk around in

16×16 tiles, integer-scaled. A local-shop corner: warm windows, a hanging sign, the player on the path.

Hero sprite & tiles (zoomed — see the pixels)

The protagonist (16×24) gets a 4-direction walk cycle; trees/props are 16-px tiles. Shown large so the pixel grid reads.

PRISM cards — pixel frames per Affinity

Each Affinity has its own pixel frame + ramp; the art window holds a pixel illustration; rarity adds a palette-cycling foil shimmer (not shown). Gloom & Order follow the same recipe.

Affinity palette ramps

Limited, saturated GBA-style ramps reused across cards, FX and UI. Dithering covers gradients — no smooth alpha washes.
