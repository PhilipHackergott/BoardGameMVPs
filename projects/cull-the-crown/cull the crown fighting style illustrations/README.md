# Cull the Crown — Fighting Style Illustrations

The **planet-card analog** for Cull the Crown. In Balatro, *Planet cards* level up a poker hand; here
they become **Fighting Styles** — the style is decided by **how many cards a play uses**, and leveling
a style scales the suit effects of plays of that size (see `../FIGHTING-STYLES.md` for the mechanic).

These are **original pixel-art sprites** (IP-clean — no Balatro art), drawn in the same
celestial/cosmic frame as the reference *Pluto* planet card: a cream physical card edge → deep cosmic
navy face → starfield → a top **name-plate** → one glowing weapon "celestial body" on a halo disc.

## How these are made
Generated by `app/scripts/pixel-art.mjs` (the same pixel pipeline as the jokers): each card is drawn
on a **60 × 84 integer pixel grid** with rasterizing primitives, emitted as run-length `<rect>`
pixels (`shape-rendering=crispEdges`). Edit the **style functions in `pixel-art.mjs`**, not the SVGs.

Pipeline: `node scripts/pixel-art.mjs` → `node scripts/raster-jokers.mjs` (rasterizes both the joker
and fighting-style folders to `.png`).

## Index
| File | Style | Played with | Motif |
|---|---|---|---|
| `two-handed.svg` | Two-Handed | 1 card | a single greatsword |
| `sword-and-shield.svg` | Sword & Shield | 2 cards | round shield + crossing sword |
| `spear.svg` | Spear | 3 cards | a long leaf-bladed spear |
| `dual-wielding.svg` | Dual Wielding | 4 cards | two crossed swords |
| `mage.svg` | Mage | 5 cards | an arcane orb-staff |
| `mounted.svg` | Mounted | any play with an Ace | a horseshoe cradling an Ace pip |

`mounted` is an **overlay** — it stacks on top of whichever size-style the play already is (a 3-card
play that includes an Ace is **Spear + Mounted**).
