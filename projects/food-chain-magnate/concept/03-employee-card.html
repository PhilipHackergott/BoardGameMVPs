Employee Card — the ONE model

Every employee in the game renders through this badge: org chart, job market, training view, tooltips. Header color = track.

Anatomy of the badge
  • Header band — role title (signage type) + tier pill; colored by track (management/recruiting/kitchen…).
  • Bust + ability — a code-drawn portrait and the one-line effect (full text on hover).
  • Foot rail — the salary chip ($5 / FREE / discounted) and the report slots (▢) + training arrow to the next role.
