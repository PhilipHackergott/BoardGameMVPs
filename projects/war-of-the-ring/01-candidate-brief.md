## The core loop
Two asymmetric sides — the **Free Peoples** and the **Shadow** — fight the War of the Ring. Each
turn both players roll a pool of **Action Dice** (Free Peoples 4, Shadow 7) and spend them one at a
time (Free Peoples first) to move and fight **armies**, advance reluctant nations along a
**political track** toward war, **muster** reinforcements and bring **characters** into play, draw
and play **event cards**, and move the hidden **Fellowship** toward Mount Doom. The Shadow funds
the **Hunt for the Ring**, piling dice into the Hunt Box to inflict **Corruption** on the
Ring-bearers. You win by **military conquest** (capture enough enemy settlements) or by the
**Ring** (destroy it at the Crack of Doom, or — as Shadow — corrupt the Ring-bearer to 12).

## Why digital
Removes the game's biggest frictions: a large board with ~200 figures and multiple tracks to set
up and maintain; honor-system hidden information (Fellowship position, Corruption, Guide); fiddly
Hunt-tile and combat-dice resolution; and a 3-hour table commitment. A digital version enforces
the rules perfectly, hides information honestly, automates upkeep, and enables save/resume and
asynchronous play.

## Top risk
**Scope.** This is among the heaviest titles in the hobby; a faithful MVP is a large engine + UI
effort, far bigger than the factory's prior titles.

## Top opportunity
Huge, devoted audience and **no fully faithful, polished, free digital version** the operator can
just play — strong pull for a clean engine-first adaptation.

## Natural player mode (note, not scored)
Effectively **2-player only** (the duel is the game). MVP ships as **local hot-seat / pass-and-play**
with engine-enforced hidden information; vs-AI and online are deferred fast-follows.

## IP first impression (note, not scored)
**Licensed property, doubly so:** the game is © **Ares Games**, and the underlying world is
**Tolkien Estate / Middle-earth Enterprises** IP. For any public release this would be `blocked`
without a license. **Operator decision (recorded at analysis):** unpublished, non-distributed
personal prototype using the real IP directly → treated as `clear` **on the basis of
non-distribution only**.

## Recommendation
**Pursue.** Top-tier demand and a near-perfect digital-fit case; proceed with eyes open that the
MVP is large and must be scoped ruthlessly (engine-first, hot-seat, base game only).
